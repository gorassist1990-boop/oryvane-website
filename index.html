ORYVANE

An original, AI-assisted music production project.

ORYVANE creates original electronic music, mainly melodic and deep house made for late hours and night drives. Other genres appear occasionally and are labelled as such.

How the music is made

Each release is produced with the help of AI tools for music generation, artwork and planning, under the direction and review of a human owner who decides what is published. ORYVANE does not present AI-assisted work as fully human-performed.

Where to listen

ORYVANE releases are published on the ORYVANE YouTube channel.

The ORYVANE publishing tool

Releases are prepared and uploaded with a private, self-hosted tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It connects to the owner's YouTube channel through Google OAuth so that it can upload videos, update their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read video statistics. The tool is not offered to the public and is used only with the owner's own Google account.

The Privacy Policy explains exactly what Google data the tool accesses, how credentials are stored and how access can be revoked. Use of this website is covered by the Terms of Service.
